How it scores

An alarm is three or more different insiders of one company, trading the same way, inside 14 days. The score says how loud it is.

RulePointsWhy
Heads30 to 5430 for three insiders, 8 more for each extra one, up to six.
Not planned0 to 20The share of insiders who traded outside a 10b5-1 plan. A planned sale was decided months ago.
Top brass10A CEO, CFO, president or chair is among them.
Tight4 or 8All trades inside nine days give 4, inside five days give 8.
Buying8Insiders sell for many reasons. They buy for one.

80 and up: the whole mob is up. 60 to 79: standing tall. Below 60: heads up.

Where the data comes from

  • TradesSEC Form 4, read from EDGAR. Only open-market buys and sells over $10K. Grants, option exercises, tax withholding and gifts are left out.
  • TokensRobinhood's stock token registry on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663). A company without a token is not watched.
  • WalletsBalances are read live from Robinhood Chain when you press Look.
  • RefreshFilings are read once a day. A Form 4 is due within two business days of the trade.

Questions

Do I need $MEERKAT to use the site?

No. Everything on it is free and needs no account.

Why are most alarms sells?

Insiders are paid in stock and sell it all the time. Several buying at once is rare, which is why it gets extra points.

Does an alarm mean the stock will move?

No. It means several people who know the company acted the same way at the same time. That is a fact from the filings, not a forecast.

Who can buy stock tokens?

Stock tokens are issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. They are not available to US persons or in several other places, including the UK and Canada.

Is this investment advice?

No. MEERKAT shows what public filings say. Following insiders can lose money.
